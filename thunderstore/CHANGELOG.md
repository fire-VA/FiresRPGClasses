## 0.1.192
- Works with Valheim 1.0.20: the extra wood and ore drops from Paragon talents work on rocks again.
- Every Paragon talent is now active. Earlier, some showed in the tree but did nothing.
- The Gemcutter talent now works.
- Player > Stats shows one line per bonus source (attributes, skills, talents, forms and Paragon stars), with Paragon totals shown correctly.
- The Classes window shows Summons only to classes that have summons, and its Achievements pill opens the hub's Achievements tab.
- With the Fires UI turned off, the possession strip and summons chip no longer flood the log.
- Cleaner, better-documented code. Gameplay is unchanged.
## 0.1.188
- Classes now lives under the Player tab in the F1 hub, and your class bonuses show skill by skill on the Player > Stats page.
- Every Paragon star now does what it says, and Paragon talents were added.
- Piercing Shot now goes through 2, 3 or 4 enemies by rank, with more damage for each; higher ranks stagger and can't be blocked.
- Ability bars can be resized, and typing in chat only hides them when the chat window is actually on top of them.
- The raven hint says "no cap" when attribute points have no limit, and the in-game help lists every admin command.
- A simpler settings screen.

## 0.1.175
- Two new race classes: the Werewolf (Wolf Form, claws, a scary Howl, the Alpha ultimate) and the Vampire (Bat Form flight, Life Drain, Blood Frenzy, Mesmerise, the Crimson Tide ultimate).
- You can catch lycanthropy or vampirism from bites and cure it with a potion or a shrine. Sunlight weakens vampires; Duskbloom Stew wards it off.
- Paragon: after the level cap you keep earning points for three star trees (Warfare, Fitness, Craft).
- Builds can grow further: soft caps instead of hard limits, and gear or talents can now lower cooldowns.
- A new Classes window and HUD; the class bars and other class HUD pieces can be moved and resized.
- Buffs and debuffs show proper names and icons.
- Lots of fixes, and a lighter HUD.

- Ten classes including Druid and Necromancer, talent trees, two ability bars and ultimates.
