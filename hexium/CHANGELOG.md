## 0.1.192
- Works with Valheim 1.0.20: the extra wood and ore drops from Paragon talents work on rocks again.
- Every Paragon talent is now active. Earlier, some showed in the tree but did nothing.
- The Gemcutter talent now works.
- Player > Stats shows one line per bonus source (attributes, skills, talents, forms and Paragon stars), with Paragon totals shown correctly.
- The Classes window shows Summons only to classes that have summons, and its Achievements pill opens the hub's Achievements tab.
- With the Fires UI turned off, the possession strip and summons chip no longer flood the log.
- Cleaner, better-documented code. Gameplay is unchanged.
## 0.1.180
- Classes is now a pill under the F1 hub's Player tab.
- Your class bonuses show on the new Player > Stats page.
- Ability bars can be resized.
- Added Paragon talents.
- The raven hint says "no cap" when attribute points have no cap.
- The in-game help lists every admin command.
- Cleaner settings screen: tuning options are under Show Advanced and window sizes are hidden.
- Piercing Shot now passes through 2, 3 or 4 enemies by rank, with +20% damage for each, and rank 3 staggers the first enemy hit.
- Piercing Shot rank 2 (Breach) makes those hits unblockable.

## 0.1.175
- Two new race classes: the Werewolf (Wolf Form, claws, a Howl that scares, staggers or weakens what hears it, the Alpha ultimate) and the Vampire (Bat Form flight, Life Drain, Blood Frenzy, Mesmerise, the Crimson Tide ultimate, sunlight, and the Duskbloom Stew that wards it off).
- Lycanthropy and vampirism can be caught from bites and cured with potions or a shrine.
- Paragon: after the level cap you keep earning points for three Champion-style star trees (Warfare, Fitness, Craft), with slottable stars.
- Builds can grow much further: attributes, Life Drain, Fury and the Monk combo use soft caps instead of hard limits, and gear or talents can now reduce cooldowns.
- A new Classes window and HUD; the class bars, summons list and possession strip can be resized and rearrange to fit, and the class bars can be moved at any time without opening the editor.
- Buffs and debuffs show proper names and icons.
- Many fixes, and a lighter HUD.
- Needs Fires Unified Core 0.2.584.
